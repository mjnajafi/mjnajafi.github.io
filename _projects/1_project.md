A project on distributed control of DC microgrids, developed between January 2021 and September 2022.

- Developed voltage restoration algorithms with precise power sharing through pinning gain control.
- Developed event-triggered algorithms to reduce communication among renewable energy resources.
- Implemented a hybrid control architecture combining continuous-time and discrete-time control.
- Designed a supervisor mechanism for dynamic controller switching, optimizing performance and stability.
